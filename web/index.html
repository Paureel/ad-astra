Scope

          

            Absolute Eulerian source energy for the fixed 2c geometry, including
            the infinite exterior tail. Values are numerical estimates, not
            certified bounds or propulsion requirements. Formal promotion
            remains unavailable.
          

          

            The wireframe marks the baseline F profile; the 8 m craft is a scale
            reference. The density field is interpolated from sampled data,
            revolved around the x axis using the benchmark’s axisymmetry. Colors
            share a fixed linear signed scale. The finite display omits
            densities below 0.005% of that scale; it does not truncate the
            energy integral.
          

          

            Moving markers use a shared coordinate time and piecewise-linear
            interpolation of the supplied finite null-ray integrations. Playback
            slows their finite time interval to a 12-second loop; it is not
            real-time travel. The stationary density field does not evolve.
            Sphere rotation in the mass comparison is illustrative.
          

          

            The annihilation comparison uses E = 2mc²: m kilograms of antimatter
            and the same mass of ordinary matter, with ideal complete conversion
            and energy recovery. It compares rest energy only; it is not a fuel
            requirement or a way to produce the geometry's stress-energy.
            Antimatter has positive rest energy; it does not supply negative
            density. Sphere volume represents mass at Earth's mean density, on a
            separate scale from the metre-based geometry. The colors distinguish
            the two masses, not their predicted appearance.
          

          

            Only supplied finite null-ray integrations are drawn. Imported files
            report their own numerical checks; the browser does not verify their
            calculations. History dates are recorded evaluation times, not
            certification dates. Local imports are saved in this browser.
