# Community conduct

Treat people with respect. Welcome contributors of different backgrounds and
experience levels. Critique claims, code, and evidence rather than people.
Credit others' work and describe uncertainty honestly. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, and sharing someone else's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors. Report conduct concerns to the repository owner
through an existing private contact channel; avoid posting personal details in
issues or discussions.
